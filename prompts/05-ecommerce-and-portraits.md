# 05. E-Commerce Product & Headshot Enhancement Prompts

All prompts below enforce strict identity, tone, and fidelity guardrails.

## 1. E-Commerce Product Photo Clarity & Crisp Packaging Labels

- **ID**: `ecom-product-clarity-label`
- **Best for**: Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, and marketplace listings with soft smartphone shots or compressed supplier photos.

```text
Enhance this product photograph to crisp, commercial e-commerce clarity while keeping the exact product geometry, brand colors, and every letter on the packaging label 100% faithful.

Enhancement priorities:
- Remove JPEG artifacts, sensor noise, and slight lens softness across packaging, glass, metal, or fabric textures.
- Balance studio exposure and clean white/neutral backdrop uniformity without blowing out subtle product shadows.
- Keep all printed brand logos, ingredient text, and SKU typography sharp and unaltered.

Strict constraints:
- Do NOT invent new label text, alter brand spelling, change product proportions, or add props.
```

## 2. Apparel & Footwear Texture Enhancement

- **ID**: `ecom-apparel-fabric-detail`
- **Best for**: Fashion listings needing realistic knit, denim, leather grain, and accurate true-to-life color.

```text
Sharpen and clean up this apparel/footwear product photograph so customers can clearly see real stitching, fabric weave, leather grain, and hardware details.

Enhancement priorities:
- Recover fine micro-texture in cotton, wool, suede, leather, and seams without harsh digital sharpening halos.
- Preserve exact garment color accuracy (critical to prevent customer returns).

Strict constraints:
- Do NOT alter garment fit, wrinkles that show drape, brand tags, or true hue.
```

## 3. Jewelry & Watch Macro Specular Cleanup

- **ID**: `ecom-jewelry-metal-clean`
- **Best for**: Rings, necklaces, and watches suffering from dust specks, micro-blur, or dull reflections.

```text
Enhance this macro jewelry/watch photograph for sharp commercial presentation while preserving exact stone facets, engravings, and dial markings.

Enhancement priorities:
- Remove stray lint, dust specks, and minor compression softness.
- Restore crisp metallic specular highlights and clean gemstone facet edges without fake starburst flares.

Strict constraints:
- Keep dial numbers, brand logos, hallmarked engravings, and stone proportions 100% accurate.
```

## 4. Natural Professional Headshot Clarity (Zero AI Glamour)

- **ID**: `portrait-linkedin-headshot-clean`
- **Best for**: LinkedIn, speaker bios, and company team pages taken in imperfect office lighting.

```text
Enhance this professional headshot for crisp focus, clean white balance, and natural office/studio lighting while preserving 100% of the person's authentic appearance.

Enhancement priorities:
- Sharpen eyes, glasses frames, hair strands, and blazer/shirt fabric naturally.
- Gently balance harsh overhead shadows and uneven skin lighting while preserving real pores, natural wrinkles, and authentic skin tone.
- Clean up minor background sensor noise while keeping natural background blur.

Strict constraints:
- Do NOT change facial structure, hair style, clothing, or apply heavy airbrushing.
```

## 5. Menu & Food Delivery Photo Clarity Boost

- **ID**: `ecom-food-photography-crisp`
- **Best for**: Restaurant menus, DoorDash/UberEats photos, and recipe blogs shot in dim dining rooms.

```text
Enhance the sharpness, appetizing natural color balance, and lighting clarity of this food photograph without making it look synthetic or over-processed.

Enhancement priorities:
- Neutralize muddy yellow/green indoor lighting casts so ingredients look fresh and true to life.
- Sharpen appetizing surface textures (crust, herbs, glaze highlights, ceramics) and reduce dim-room ISO noise.

Strict constraints:
- Do NOT add new garnishes, alter portion size, or apply unnatural neon saturation.
```

## 6. Reduce Eyeglass Lens Reflection & Recover Eyes

- **ID**: `portrait-glass-glare-fix`
- **Best for**: Portraits where screen glare or camera flash partially obscures eyes behind glasses.

```text
Gently reduce harsh white/green glare reflections on the eyeglass lenses in this portrait to improve eye visibility while preserving the subject's exact eyes, lens frames, and natural lighting.

Strict constraints:
- Keep subtle realistic glass reflections so the lenses still look genuine—do NOT delete the glasses.
- Preserve exact eye color, gaze direction, eyelid shape, and frame geometry.
```
